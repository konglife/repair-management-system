Top recommendation — tackle first

C1 · One EntityPicker module

It is the cleanest "deepening": today there are two shallow adapters (Product/Parts, 95% duplicated) and a missing Customer picker — three call sites, three shapes. One deep module gives one interface across all three, and it lands directly on the owner's #1 stated pain (pickers are hard to use, entry speed is everything in the evening batch). Low risk, pure leverage, repair-first.
